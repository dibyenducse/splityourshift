What makes SplitYourShift different

Split any shift, any day

Cut a shift on any hour into up to four parts and give each part to a different worker, just for that day. Overnight shifts too. Each part works with the time clock, swaps and payroll.

One-tap time clock

Workers clock in and out from their phone with one tap. You set the grace period; late arrivals, staying late and forgotten clock-outs are handled with simple choices.

Payroll that adds itself up

Weekly, every two weeks or monthly for each business: hours from the time clock or from the schedule, overtime, bonuses, deductions and printable payslips.

Shift swaps without phone calls

Workers ask a coworker to take a shift and see who is free. When they agree, the schedule updates and the owner is told.

Everything else

  • Drag-and-drop weekly schedule for each location, on a computer or a phone
  • Weekly shift limits and availability checks
  • Time-off requests with a list of who can cover
  • Shifts owed after emergency covers, paid back on any later date
  • Shift report logbook and task lists for each shift
  • Each worker gets their own login with their shifts and pay
